# code_3000
Collection of Assignments for CSE 3000: Contemporary Issues in Computer Science and Engineering


Compassion:
Why: I believe that technology and work with it must benefit human well-being. If a job didn't ground itself in the reality of people, it would fail its purpose of providing equitable outcomes. 

What: I would never support or work on deploying something at an expense of a person's mental health or safety. 

How: I would consistently advocate for acknowledgement and discussion of ethical concerns when working on a project. I would actively seek out and listen to the concerns of various stakeholders to ensure the human impact of the work is addressed. 


Transparency:
Why: Transparency is the foundation of any relationship or product. Transparency is used to build honesty and trust that surrounds something. People should not be manipulated by systems that can affect their lives.

What: I commit to clear communication, acknowledging any disclaimers for a product that may not be up to standard. I will also be fully clear with legalities and the processes of how the system works. People will not be decieved for the tools they choose to use to ensure trust and honesty. 

How: I advocate for understandable explanations on how various points of data are collected and used. Along with what they should know and when they need to know. Documentation will be a strong point in holding myself and others accountable for making any system or product development practices clear to anyone.
